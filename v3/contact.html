Quick answers

How do I track my order?

You will get an SMS when your order is dispatched. For anything else, call the hotline with your order number and we will check for you.

How do I claim warranty?

Bring or send your invoice and the product's serial number to any showroom, or choose "Warranty claim" in the form above. We will register the claim and coordinate with the brand's service centre.

Can I apply for Easy Payment over the phone?

You can start the application online or at a showroom. Our team may call you to verify details before approval. See Easy Payment.

Do you deliver outside Colombo?

Yes, islandwide. Delivery time depends on the product and your district. See delivery policy.
