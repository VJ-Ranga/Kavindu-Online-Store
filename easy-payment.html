Easy Payment: pay for it monthly

Kavindu's own instalment plan for furniture, appliances, TVs and laptops priced Rs 15,000 and above. Pay a down payment of 20% or more, then settle the balance in equal monthly amounts over 6 to 36 months. No credit card needed.

How Easy Payment works

  1. 1

    Pick a product and a plan

    Choose any item of Rs 15,000 or more. Select your down payment and the number of months. The calculator below shows the exact monthly amount.

  2. 2

    Apply online

    Fill in the application with your NIC, income details and four documents. It takes about 10 minutes. A guarantor is optional.

  3. 3

    We review within 1 working day

    Our credit team checks your documents and calls you on the mobile number you give. You pay the down payment once you are approved.

  4. 4

    Delivery, then monthly payments

    We deliver and install. Pay each month by card on PayHere, bank transfer or at any showroom.

The cash price is always the lowest price. An Easy Payment plan costs more because the balance is paid over time. Rates shown are indicative and final terms are set out in your agreement.

Eligibility and documents

Who can apply

  • Sri Lankan citizen aged 21 to 60 with a valid NIC
  • Regular monthly income, salaried or self-employed, at least 3 times the monthly instalment
  • Delivery address within our island-wide delivery area
  • A mobile number registered in your own name
  • Down payment of at least 20% of the cash price

Documents you need

  • NIC, front and back (clear photo or scan)
  • Utility bill from the last 3 months showing your address
  • Salary slip for the last month, or 3 months bank statements if self-employed
  • Guarantor NIC only if you choose to add a guarantor

Files must be JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB each.
