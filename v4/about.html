About Kavindu

Furniture and home appliances for Sri Lankan homes, sold through our showrooms and online, with Easy Payment for families who prefer to pay monthly.

Who we are

Kavindu Enterprises is a Sri Lankan retailer of home and office furniture, home appliances, televisions and computers. We started with a single shop in Avissawella and now serve customers through six showrooms and this online store.

Most of our customers are families furnishing a first home, replacing a worn-out fridge, or setting up a study room for the children. We keep the range practical: products that suit local homes, are covered by a proper warranty, and can be serviced if something goes wrong.

Many families also tell us they cannot pay the full price at once. That is why we run our own Easy Payment plan, so you can take the product home now and pay in monthly instalments.

Why buy from Kavindu

Genuine warranty

Branded products come with the manufacturer or authorised-agent warranty. Your invoice is your proof of purchase, and we help you through the claim if you need to make one.

Delivery and installation

Islandwide delivery, and installation for air conditioners, washing machines, wall-mounted TVs and larger furniture. Free delivery in the Colombo area on orders over Rs 50,000.

Easy Payment

Instalments from 6 to 36 months on purchases over Rs 15,000, with a down payment from 20%. No credit card required. See how it works.

After-sales support

If something is wrong, call the hotline or visit any showroom. We log the issue, coordinate with the brand's service centre and keep you updated.
