Delivery

Where we deliver

We deliver to all districts in Sri Lanka. Delivery to some remote areas may take longer or may need to be arranged with our team.

Delivery times

AreaItems in stock

Delivery charges

Delivery is free for Colombo-area orders over Rs 50,000. Other orders are charged a flat Rs 1,500, or the rate shown at checkout for bulky items and distant locations.

Installation

We offer installation for air conditioners, washing machines, wall-mounted TVs and assembly of larger furniture. Installation is scheduled with you after delivery, and any extra materials (for example copper piping for an air conditioner) are quoted before work starts.

On delivery

Please check the packaging and the product in front of our delivery team and report any damage straight away. Someone aged 18 or over must be present to receive and sign for the order.

Returns & refunds

7-day return for unopened items

You may return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unopened, unused, in its original sealed packaging, and comes with the invoice. We will arrange collection or you can bring it to a showroom.

Items that cannot be returned

  • Products that have been opened, installed or used, unless faulty
  • Made-to-order or customised furniture
  • Mattresses once the seal is removed
  • Items marked as clearance or final sale

Faulty or damaged items

If an item arrives damaged or has a fault, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order number. We will repair, replace or refund it, depending on the stock position and the nature of the fault. Faults that appear later are handled under the warranty.

Refunds

Approved refunds are paid back by the original payment method within 7 to 14 working days after we receive and inspect the item. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

Warranty

Most products carry a manufacturer or authorised-agent warranty. The length is shown on each product page and on your invoice.

Making a claim

  1. Keep your invoice and the product serial number.
  2. Contact the hotline, any showroom or use the contact form with the topic "Warranty claim".
  3. We register the claim and coordinate with the brand's service centre. Some brands will ask you to take the product to their service centre directly.

What is not covered

Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, power surges, water or pest damage, unauthorised repair, normal wear (for example fabric wear on furniture), or cosmetic damage after delivery. Specific terms are set by each manufacturer.

Our own Kavindu-branded products are covered by the warranty period stated on the product page. Statutory consumer rights under Sri Lankan law are not affected.

Privacy

Kavindu Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd processes personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka.

What we collect

Name, phone number, email, delivery address, order history and, for Easy Payment applications, NIC number, employment and income details and supporting documents. Card payments are handled by the payment provider and we do not store card numbers.

Why we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders and provide after-sales service
  • To assess and manage Easy Payment applications
  • To contact you about your order, and about offers if you have agreed to receive them
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

Sharing

We share data only with delivery partners, payment providers and brand service centres where needed to complete your order, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent for marketing, or ask how your data is used. Write to hello@kavindustore.lk. We keep records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Easy Payment terms

Easy Payment is Kavindu's own instalment plan. A summary of the terms is below; the full agreement is given to you to sign before delivery.

Approval

Applications are subject to approval. We may ask for your NIC, proof of income and address, and a guarantor. We may decline an application without giving a reason. Products are delivered only after approval and receipt of the down payment.

Down payment

A down payment of at least 20% of the cash price is required. The balance, plus the plan charge for the chosen period (6 to 36 months), is paid in equal monthly instalments.

Ownership

The product remains the property of Kavindu Enterprises until the final instalment is paid. Ownership passes to you on full payment. You may not sell or pledge the product until then.

Late payment

A late payment fee may apply to overdue instalments, as set out in your signed agreement. If instalments remain unpaid after reminders, we may take steps to recover the product or the balance as the agreement and law allow.

Early settlement

You may settle early. Any rebate on the plan charge is explained in your agreement.
